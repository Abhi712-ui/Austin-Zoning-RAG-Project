(A)

A corner store special use is limited to the following commercial uses:

(1)

art gallery;

(2)

consumer convenience services;

(3)

consumer repair services;

(4)

food sales;

(5)

general retail sales (convenience);

(6)

personal services;

(7)

restaurant (general); and

(8)

restaurant (limited).

(B)

A maximum of one dwelling unit is permitted in a corner store structure.

Source: Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 031211-11; Ord. 040617-Z-1.
