The purpose of a planned development area (PDA) combining district is to:

(1)

provide for industrial and commercial uses in certain commercial and industrial base districts; or

(2)

incorporate the terms of a planned development area agreement into a zoning ordinance following annexation of a property that is subject to a planned development area agreement.

Source: Section 13-2-150; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
