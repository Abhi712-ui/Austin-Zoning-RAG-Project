(A)

This section applies to a printing and publishing use in a community commercial (GR) or more restrictive district.

(B)

The use may not:

(1)

exceed 5,000 square feet of gross floor area; or

(2)

produce external noise, vibration, smoke, dust, odor, heat, glare, fumes, electrical interference, or waste runoff.

(C)

The following are prohibited:

(1)

the outdoor storage of materials; and

(2)

the wholesale distribution of goods.

(D)

The use is limited to printing equipment typically used in a business office.

Source: Ord. 040617-Z-1.
