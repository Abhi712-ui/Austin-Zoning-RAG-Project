In this division:

(1)

MOBILE HOME SPACE means an area in a mobile home park that is designed for and designated as the location for a single mobile home and the exclusive use of its occupants.

(2)

MOBILE HOME STAND means that portion of a mobile home space on which the mobile home is placed.

Source: Section 13-2-930; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
