(A)

A person may not use or develop property as a residential infill or neighborhood urban center special use unless the Planning Commission approves a development plan under this division.

(B)

A development plan must include:

(1)

the locations of land uses, number of dwelling units, and approximate gross floor area of each use;

(2)

the layout of the transportation network;

(3)

the location, size, and type of each community open space area;

(4)

the location and type of each drainage or water quality control facility;

(5)

the location of the 100-year flood plain;

(6)

the location of each critical environmental feature; and

(7)

additional information required by the director of the Neighborhood Planning and Zoning Department to demonstrate compliance with this subchapter.

Source: Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 010329-18; Ord. 031211-11.
