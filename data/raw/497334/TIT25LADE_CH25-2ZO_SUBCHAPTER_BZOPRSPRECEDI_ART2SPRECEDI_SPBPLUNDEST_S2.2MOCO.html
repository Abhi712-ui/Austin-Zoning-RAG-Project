The proposed development must comply with the requirements of this code, except that:

A.

the council may modify a requirement in accordance with Section 2.5. (Development Bonuses); and

B.

the council may waive or modify a requirement if:

1.

the PUD ordinance identifies the waiver or modification; and

2.

the council finds that:

a.

the resulting development would achieve greater consistency with the goals enumerated in Section 1.1 (General Intent) than development that would occur without the waiver or modification; and

b.

the adverse effects of the waiver or modification are offset by other enforceable requirements; and

c.

the objective of the waived or modified requirement is substantially achieved.

Source: Ord. 20080618-098.
