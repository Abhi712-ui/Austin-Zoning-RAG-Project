(A)

This section applies to a building in a general office (GO), neighborhood commercial (LR), general commercial services (CS), or commercial services - liquor sales (CS-1) district that:

(1)

is located on property adjacent to an Lake Austin residence (LA), rural residence (RR), or single-family residence large lot (SF-1) district; and

(2)

exceeds a height of 35 feet.

(B)

A building's exterior glass is required to be either clear or lightly tinted.

(C)

Exterior light illuminating a building above the second floor is prohibited.

Source: Section 13-2-607; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
