In this article:

SCENIC VISTA means a generally recognizable, noteworthy view of:

(1)

Barton Creek;

(2)

Bull Creek;

(3)

West Bull Creek;

(4)

Lake Austin;

(5)

Lake Travis;

(6)

a valley of the Colorado River; or

(7)

the downtown area of Austin.

Source: Section 13-2-783(b)(1); Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
