(A)

The purpose of the East Sixth/Pecan Street (PS) overlay district is to protect the historic character of East Sixth/Pecan Street and to enhance the pedestrian environment of the area.

(B)

The PS overlay district applies to all property zoned CBD or DMU that is located between the alleys and the extension of alley lines on each side of and parallel to East Sixth Street from IH-35 to the first alley east of and parallel to Congress Avenue.

Source: Section 13-2-173; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
