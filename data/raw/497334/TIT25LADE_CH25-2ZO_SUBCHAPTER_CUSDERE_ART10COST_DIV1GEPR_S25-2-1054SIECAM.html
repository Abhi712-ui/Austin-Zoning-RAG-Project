(A)

Except as provided in Subsection (B), council may grant site-specific amendments to height and compatibility buffers if council determines that an amendment is appropriate and will not harm the surrounding area.

(B)

A site is not eligible for a site-specific amendment if the site is:

(1)

zoned:

(a)

a special purpose base zoning district;

(b)

density bonus (DB) combining district; or

(2)

subject to the university neighborhood overlay (UNO).

(C)

A site-specific amendment to this article is considered a rezoning of property and is subject to the same requirements and procedures established for a rezoning application that changes the base district classification of a property.

(D)

A site-specific amendment may be initiated by:

(1)

council;

(2)

the Land Use Commission; or

(3)

the record owner.

Source: Ord. No. 20240516-004, Pt. 1, 7-15-24.
