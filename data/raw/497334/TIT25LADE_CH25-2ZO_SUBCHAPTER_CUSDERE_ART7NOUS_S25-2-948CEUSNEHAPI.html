(A)

This section applies to a use that is nonconforming because of its proximity to a hazardous pipeline under Section 25-2-516(B) (Development Near a Hazardous Pipeline).

(B)

Proximity to a hazardous pipeline does not cause a structure or area to be noncomplying under Article 8 (Noncomplying Structures).

(C)

Except as provided by Subsection (C), the use is governed by Group "D" regulations prescribed by Section 25-2-947 (Nonconforming Use Regulation Groups).

(D)

If there is a conflict between the regulations prescribed by this section and the regulations as determined by Section 25-2-946 (Determination of Nonconforming Use Regulation Group), the more restrictive regulations apply.

Source: Ord. 030410-12; Ord. 031211-11.
