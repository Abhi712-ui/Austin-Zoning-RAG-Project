(A)

Except as otherwise provided in this section, a person may not place, maintain, or occupy a mobile home in the city other than in a mobile home park that is licensed under this division, or in a mobile home subdivision.

(B)

This section does not apply to a mobile home that is:

(1)

located on a construction site and is used as a field office during construction;

(2)

a mobile home sales lot; or

(3)

a mobile home that is under construction or stored at a mobile home manufacturing plant.

Source: Section 13-2-934; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
