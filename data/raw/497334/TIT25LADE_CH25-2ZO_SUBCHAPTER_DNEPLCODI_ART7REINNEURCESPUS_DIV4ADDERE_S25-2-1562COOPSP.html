In addition to other community open space requirements prescribed by this subchapter:

(1)

each community open space area must be at least 500 square feet in size and at least 20 feet across in each direction;

(2)

the aggregate impervious cover for all community open spaces may not exceed 50 percent; and

(3)

a plaza or square may not exceed 90 percent impervious cover.

Source: Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 031211-11.
