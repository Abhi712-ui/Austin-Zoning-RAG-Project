(A)

Before an application can be reviewed by the Land Use Commission, the director of the Planning Department must obtain recommendations from the directors of the following departments:

(1)

Economic Development Department; and

(2)

Housing Department.

(B)

The Land Use Commission shall consider the criteria established in Section 25-2-452 (Applications and Criteria for District Designation) when reviewing an application to designate a creative spaces district.
