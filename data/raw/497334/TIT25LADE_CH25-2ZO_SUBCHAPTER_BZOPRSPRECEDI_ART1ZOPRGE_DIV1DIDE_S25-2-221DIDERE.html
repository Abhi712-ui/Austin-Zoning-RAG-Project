(A)

All land within the zoning jurisdiction shall be designated as a named zoning base district in accordance with the procedures of state law and this subchapter. Different portions of a site may be designated as different zoning base districts, but only one zoning base district designation may apply to any portion of a site.

(B)

A zoning combining district designation may be applied to property in addition to the zoning base district designation.

Source: Section 13-2-24; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
