(A)

In the Capitol view corridor (CVC) combining district, the maximum height permitted is the lesser of:

(1)

the base district maximum height; or

(2)

the maximum height provided in this section.

(B)

In a Capitol view corridor, a structure may not exceed the elevation of the plane delineating the corridor. The height limitation exceptions of Section 25-2-531 (Height Limitation Exceptions) do not apply to this subsection.
