(A)

This section applies in a warehouse/limited office (W/LO) district.

(B)

The building must include an office use. The minimum floor area for the office use is the lesser of:

(1)

20 percent; or

(2)

1,000 square feet.

(C)

A truck loading dock may not be located on the same building face as an office entrance.

(D)

An office use must face the street that provides primary access.

(E)

A construction sales and service use may not exceed 10,000 square feet of gross floor area.

(F)

The Land Use Commission may approve, in accordance with the applicable provisions of Chapter 25-5, Article 3 (Land Use Commission Approved Site Plans), the following modifications to the site development regulations:

(1)

a lot that contains at least 21,780 square feet, but less than 43,560 square feet;

(2)

a structure with a height greater than 25 feet, but not more than 35 feet; or

(3)

a structure with a floor area ratio of more than 0.25, but not more than 0.50.

Source: Section 13-2-662; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 991104-46; Ord. 010607-8; Ord. 031211-11.
