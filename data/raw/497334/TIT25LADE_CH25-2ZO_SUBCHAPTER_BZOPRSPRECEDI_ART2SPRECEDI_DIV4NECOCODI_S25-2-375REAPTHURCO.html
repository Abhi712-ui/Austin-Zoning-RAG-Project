If an owner of property in a proposed NC combining district files a written statement protesting the inclusion of the owner's property in an NC combining district, a separate affirmative vote of three-fourths of all members of the council is required to include the protesting owner's property in an NC combining district.

Source: Sections 13-2-136 and 13-2-137; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
