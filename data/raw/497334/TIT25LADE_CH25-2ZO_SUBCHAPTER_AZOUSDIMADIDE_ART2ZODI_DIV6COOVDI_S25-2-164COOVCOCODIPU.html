(A)

The purpose of a conditional overlay (CO) combining district is to modify use and site development regulations to address the specific circumstances presented by a site.

(B)

A CO combining district may be used to:

(1)

promote compatibility between competing or potentially incompatible uses;

(2)

ease the transition from one base district to another;

(3)

address land uses or sites with special requirements; and

(4)

guide development in unique circumstances.

Source: Section 13-2-120; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
