(A)

Until council approves a station area plan in accordance with Subpart C (Station Area Plan):

(1)

a transit oriented development (TOD) district functions as an overlay district; and

(2)

property within the TOD district:

(a)

is subject to Subpart B (Initial District Regulations); and

(b)

retains its base district zoning.

(B)

The approval by council of a station area plan in accordance with Subpart C (Station Area Plan) is a rezoning of the property as a TOD base district. After the rezoning, Subpart B (Initial District Regulations) does not apply.

Source: Ord. 20050519-008.
