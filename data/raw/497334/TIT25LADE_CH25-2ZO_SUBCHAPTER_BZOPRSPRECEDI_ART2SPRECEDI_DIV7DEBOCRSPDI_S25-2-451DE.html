In this division,

(1)

COCKTAIL LOUNGE means a cocktail lounge use described in Chapter 25-2 (Zoning) and established on or before October 31, 2024.

(2)

CREATIVE SPACE means a use described in Chapter 25-2 (Zoning) that allows one or more of the following occupancies:

(a)

art gallery;

(b)

art workshop;

(c)

cocktail lounge;

(d)

cultural services:

(e)

performance venue;

(f)

personal improvement services; or

(g)

theater.

(3)

CREATIVE SPACES DISTRICT means an area designated and zoned as density bonus creative spaces (DBCS) combining district.

Source: Ord. No. 20241010-034, Pt. 3, 10-21-24.
