(A)

If vegetation in an area in which clearing is prohibited by Section 25-2-1023 (Roadway Vegetative Buffer) has been substantially disturbed, it must be revegetated with native trees, shrubs, and grasses.

(B)

Not more than 50 percent of the area in which clearing is prohibited may be used for detention or sedimentation ponds or wastewater drain fields.

Source: Section 13-7-66(b)(1); Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
