(A)

The purpose of the East Austin (EA) overlay district is to reduce the concentration of intensive commercial and industrial uses in close proximity to residential areas in East Austin and to mitigate the effect of commercial and industrial uses on nearby residential uses.

(B)

Except as provided in Subsection (C), the EA overlay district applies to property located in the area bounded by Interregional Highway 35, Airport Boulevard, and Town Lake.

(C)

The EA overlay district does not apply to land included in a neighborhood plan combining district.

Source: Section 13-2-190; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 031211-11.
