(A)

The purpose of the Barton Springs Zone (BSZ) overlay district is to preserve the natural beauty of the Hill Country, protect the image and character of the neighborhoods in the district, and reduce the negative effects of urbanization by restricting the scale and intensity of retail development.

(B)

The BSZ overlay district applies to the portion of the Barton Springs Zone, as described in Section 25-8-2 (Descriptions of Regulated Areas), that is within the city's zoning jurisdiction.

Source: Ord. 031204-57; Ord. 031211-11.
