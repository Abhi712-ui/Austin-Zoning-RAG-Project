(A)

This section applies to a single-family residential use, a duplex residential use, or a two-family residential use.

(B)

In this section, "porch" means a porch that is open on three sides and that may have a roof.

(C)

Subject to the limitations of this section, a porch may project into a front yard.

(D)

Except as provided in Subsection (E), the porch must be at least 15 feet from the front lot line.

(E)

A porch roof overhang or porch step must be at least 13 feet from the front lot line.

Source: Ord. 030925-64; Ord. 031211-11
