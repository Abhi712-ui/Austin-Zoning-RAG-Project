(A)

This section applies to a single-family residential use, a duplex residential use, or a two-family residential use.

(B)

Except as provided in Subsection (C), impervious cover in a front yard may not exceed 40 percent.

(C)

The director may waive Subsection (B) if the director determines backing a motor vehicle onto the adjacent roadway is unsafe and that a circular driveway or turnaround in the front yard is required.

(D)

Not more than four parking spaces may be located in the front street yard, or for a corner lot, not more than four parking spaces may be located in the front street yard and side street yard combined.

Source: Ord. 030925-64; Ord. 031211-11.
