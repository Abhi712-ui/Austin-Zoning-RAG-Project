There are 13 commercial district designations. The designation established for a proposed commercial use is based on:

(1)

type of use;

(2)

customer base;

(3)

location;

(4)

surrounding uses;

(5)

traffic generation; and

(6)

other factors determined to be relevant by the council.

Source: Sections 13-2-60 through 13-2-73; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11.
