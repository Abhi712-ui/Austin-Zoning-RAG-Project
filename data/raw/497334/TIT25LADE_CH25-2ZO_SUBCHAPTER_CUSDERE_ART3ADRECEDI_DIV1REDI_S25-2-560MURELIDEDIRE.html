(A)

This section applies in a multifamily residence limited density (MF-1) district.

(B)

The minimum site area for each dwelling unit is:

(1)

2,500 square feet, for an efficiency dwelling unit;

(2)

3,000 square feet, for a one bedroom dwelling unit; and

(3)

3,500 square feet, for a dwelling unit with two or more bedrooms.

Source: Section 13-2-638; Ord. 990225-70; Ord. 031211-11; Ord. 20111215-096.
