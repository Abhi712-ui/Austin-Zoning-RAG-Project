(A)

For an art gallery use in a general office (GO) or more restrictive district, the sale of art supplies, equipment, or accessories is prohibited.

(B)

This subsection applies to an art workshop use in a community commercial (GR) or more restrictive district.

(1)

The use may not:

(a)

exceed 5,000 square feet of gross floor area; or

(b)

produce external noise, vibration, smoke, dust, odor, heat, glare, fumes, electrical interference, or waste runoff.

(2)

The following are prohibited:

(a)

the outdoor storage of materials; and

(b)

the use of welding equipment, fiberglass, or epoxy.

Source: Ord. 040617-Z-1.
