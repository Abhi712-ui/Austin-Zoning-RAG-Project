(A)

A new road within a residential infill or neighborhood urban center special use project must comply with the roadway design standards of the Traditional Neighborhood District Criteria Manual.

(B)

The director of the Neighborhood Planning and Zoning Department may approve the use of an innovative roadway design that is not described in the Traditional Neighborhood District Criteria Manual.

Source: Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 010329-18; Ord. 031211-11.
