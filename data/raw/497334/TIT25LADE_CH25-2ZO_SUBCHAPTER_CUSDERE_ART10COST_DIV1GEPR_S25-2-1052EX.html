This article does not apply to:

(1)

a structural alteration that does not increase the square footage, area, or height of a building;

(2)

a site zoned central business district (CBD) or downtown mixed-use (DMU); or

(3)

a site that is used for:

(a)

duplex use;

(b)

single-family attached residential use;

(c)

single-family residential use;

(d)

small lot single-family residential use;

(e)

two-unit residential use;

(f)

three-unit residential use;

(g)

adult care services use (limited or general); or

(h)

child care services use (limited or general).

Source: Ord. No. 20240516-004, Pt. 1, 7-15-24.
