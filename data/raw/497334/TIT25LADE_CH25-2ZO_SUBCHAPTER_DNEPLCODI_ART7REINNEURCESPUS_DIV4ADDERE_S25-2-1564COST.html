(A)

The compatibility standards of Chapter 25-2, Article 10 (Compatibility Standards) apply only to property along the perimeter of an area used or developed as a residential infill or neighborhood urban center special use.

(B)

Within an area used or developed as a residential infill or neighborhood urban center special use, the compatibility standards of Section 25-3-86 (Compatibility Standards) apply.

Source: Ord. 000406-81; Ord. 031211-11.
